Getting started
  1. Add your locations: the places parts are kept, such as a shelf, a tote, a bin or a van. Go to Locations › + Add location, or just type a new location name the first time you add stock.
  2. Add a part: Parts › + Add part. Enter the P/N (part number), a description, a category and the unit value. You can enter a starting quantity and where it's kept.
  3. Print labels for your locations (see Labels and printing) and stick them on.
  4. From then on, scan a label to see what's in it, and use Book out whenever parts go to a job.
Finding parts
  • Type in the search box on the Parts tab. It searches the P/N, description, category and location names as you type. Every word must match, so resistor 10k finds parts containing both words.
  • Use the category and location drop-downs to narrow the list.
  • Tap a column heading (P/N, Qty, Value…) to sort; tap again to reverse.
  • On a computer, press / to jump to the search box.
  • A quantity in red means none are in stock.
Parts, photos and categories
  • Tap a part to open it. You'll see its photos, how many are at each location, and everything that's happened to it.
  • Photos: tap + Add photos. On a phone you can take a photo or choose several from your gallery. Tap a small photo to view it, and Make main to use it as the part's picture in lists. Tap the large photo to open it full size. Each part can have up to 20 photos.
  • Categories group similar parts (e.g. Resistors, Fasteners). Set one with Edit: pick an existing category or type a new one.
  • Edit changes the P/N, description, category or value. Changing the value doesn't change the value of parts already booked to jobs.
  • Delete removes the part and its stock. Jobs it was booked to keep their record of it.
Receiving, booking out, moving and counting stock
  • Receive: new stock has arrived. Choose the location, or type a new location name to create it.
  • Book out: parts are going to a job. Choose which location they come from and the job. Type a new job number to create the job. You can't book out more than that location has.
  • Move: parts moved from one location to another.
  • Count: after a stocktake, enter how many are actually there and the quantity is corrected.
  • Return: unused parts come back from a job. Open the job and tap Return next to the part.
  • Add a note to any of these; it shows in the history.
Locations and containers
  • Tap a location (or scan its label) to see everything in it, with quantities and value. You can book out, move, or Add stock here from that screen.
  • Each location has a label code such as LOC-0003. You can change it with Edit (e.g. to match labels you already have), but then reprint the label.
  • A location can only be deleted when it's empty.
Jobs
  • The Jobs tab lists jobs with the parts booked to them and their total value.
  • Open a job to see its parts, Book part out to it, Return unused parts, or Export CSV (e.g. for invoicing).
  • Values are fixed at the time parts are booked out.
  • Close job when it's finished; closed jobs can't have more parts booked to them. You can reopen a job.
Labels and printing (Brother P-touch)
  • Tap Print label on a location or part, or Print labels on the Locations or Parts tab to do several at once (untick the ones you don't want).
  • Choose your tape width and the code type:
    • QR code (recommended): scan with a phone's camera.
    • Barcode (Code 128): for handheld scanners that can't read QR codes.
  • Print sends the labels to a P-touch connected to a computer. In the print window, set margins to None and scale to 100%.
  • Download PNG saves label images you can print from P-touch Editor (Insert › Picture) or Brother's apps.
  • P-touch Editor CSV exports label data for P-touch Editor's database merge.
  • Phones can't usually print straight to a P-touch from a web browser. Print labels from a computer, or download the PNG on your phone.
Scanning labels
  • Phone: open your camera app and point it at a QR label, then tap the link that appears. The app opens on that location. Sign in first if asked.
  • Handheld barcode scanner (USB or Bluetooth): just scan while the app is open; you don't need to click anything first.
  • Scan button: type a location code, P/N or job number to jump straight to it.
History
  • The History tab lists every stock change: when, what, where, which job, who did it, and any note.
  • Search it by P/N, job, location, person or note.
Importing and exporting (CSV / Excel)
  • Export CSV on the Parts tab downloads a spreadsheet of stock: one row per part per location. It follows your current search and filters.
  • Import CSV adds or updates parts from a spreadsheet saved as CSV. The first row must have column names; part_number is required, and description, category, location, quantity and unit_value are optional.
  • A quantity on import sets the count at that location (like a stocktake). Rows with problems are skipped and listed.
People, passwords and backups
  • Tap your name (top right) for the account menu.
  • + Add person gives someone their own sign-in. Remove stops them signing in; their history stays.
  • Change your password at the bottom of the menu. This signs you out on your other devices.
  • Download backup saves a zip of all data and photos. The server also keeps daily backups, but keep your own copy somewhere else (e.g. Google Drive) every so often.
  • Forgotten password? Nobody can look up a password, but whoever manages the server can set a new one (see the hosting guide).
Updating the app
  • When a new version is available, a dot appears on your name. Open the menu to see what's changed under Updates.
  • Tap Update now. Your data is backed up first, the app restarts (about a minute), and the page reloads itself.
  • Updates never change your parts, stock, jobs or photos.
Using it on your phone
  • In your phone's browser menu, choose Add to Home Screen. The app then opens from an icon, like any other app.
  • Everything works on a phone: searching, booking out, taking photos and scanning labels.
Something's not working
  • "Please sign in" keeps appearing: your sign-in expired (after 30 days) or your password was changed on another device. Sign in again.
  • "Too many failed sign-ins": wait 15 minutes and try again.
  • A label won't scan: make sure it's flat and well lit. Phone cameras read QR codes; for a handheld 1D scanner, print Code 128 labels.
  • A photo won't upload: photos must be JPEG, PNG, GIF or WebP, up to 10 MB (phone photos are shrunk automatically).
  • Can't delete a location: it still has stock. Move or book out the stock first.
  • The site won't load at all: whoever manages the server can check it using the "If something doesn't work" section of the hosting guide.
